Disconnecting means
An attachment plug and receptacle may serve as the sole disconnecting means if the manual controls are within 1.8 m (6 ft) of the floor, or an approved manual switch is installed in sight of the unit.
Supply-cord protection
The manufacturer must fit a Leakage-Current Detector-Interrupter (LCDI), Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter (AFCI), or Heat-Detecting Circuit Interrupter (HDCI), integral to the plug or within 300 mm of it.

3 — Circuits on this panel
Feeder sizing uses NEC 430.24: 125% of the largest unit's FLA/RLC + the sum of the rated currents of every other unit on the panel.
3 — HVAC Panel Load
HVAC Panel Main Breaker Calculation: calculates main breaker using total panel load in kVA. I = S × 1000 / (√3 × V) for 3-phase (or S × 1000 / V for 1-phase). Main breaker is selected with a 125% safety factor (I_125 = I × 1.25), and feeder cable ampacity is sized at Breaker / 0.80 from Elsewedy Cables LV catalogue.
3 — Motors Panel (MCC) Load
Motors Panel (MCC) Main Breaker Calculation: Enter total panel load in kVA, kW, or HP. Current is calculated as I = S × 1000 / (√3 × V) for 3-phase. Sized with 125% safety factor for continuous motor load feeder (I_125 = I × 1.25), and feeder cable ampacity is sized at Breaker / 0.80 from Elsewedy Cables LV catalogue with AutoCAD drawing tag.
